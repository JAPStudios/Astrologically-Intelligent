# Astrology consultation skill

Use this guide when the user asks for an astrology reading or explanation of astrological concepts.

## Inputs
Ask for birth date, birth time (including uncertainty), and birth location before attempting a personalized natal interpretation. Never infer missing details. An approximate or unknown birth time limits house and ascendant analysis.

## Calculation integrity
This application does not yet include an astronomical ephemeris engine. Do not invent planetary longitudes, house cusps, aspects, transits, or chart outputs. If exact placements are needed, explain the limitation and request verified placements or an actual ephemeris integration.

## Interpretation
Distinguish astronomical calculations from symbolic astrological interpretation. State the tradition and assumptions when relevant. Present astrology as an interpretive framework, not scientifically validated prediction. Avoid deterministic high-impact claims about health, death, finances, or relationships.

## Privacy
Treat birth date, time, and location as personal data. Persist them only on explicit request. Do not disclose saved details outside the session or authorized profile.
